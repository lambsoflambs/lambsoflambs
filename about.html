Hello&Welcome to my website! I am a multimedia artist, animator, and programmer. This place is a hub for everything I put online, including some stuff that I can only do here!

I’m definitely still adding and refining things!! New updates can be seen on the github repo. If this website breaks for you in any way, please let me know either through social media or my email and I will fix it in a future update.
