TYPES OF OATS

So like, IN MY OPINION, rolled oats (or “old fashioned” oats) are pretty much always the way to go. Instant oats are like, fine, but I just don’t prefer the texture. And if I can be real, steel cut oats are just too inconvenient to ever be worth it.

Here are some basic ways to make oatmeal:

Boiled oats - "you boil them"

  • Use a 1 to 4 oat-to-water ratio. 1 cup of oats should use 4 cups of water
  • Lightly salt your water
  • Once it’s boiling, dump your oats in. Instant oats take 1 minute, rolled oats take 5, and steel cut oats take 30 (if you're the kind of person who wants to wait 30 minutes for oatmeal).
  • Strain it when you’re done
  • That’s it. You can now flavor it however you like by adding anything you like!!

Overnight oats - dump your oats in a jar and cover with milk

  • Dump your oats in literally any container (it should probably have a lid). Like a jar or a tupperware or whatever.
  • Cover your oats with either water or any kind of milk (i like soy).
  • Lightly salt it
  • That’s it!!!!!!! You can now flavor it by adding whatever you want to it. Put it in the fridge overnight, grab it in the morning and just start eating!!!!
  • I make this in like 15 minutes at 2 AM while half-asleep once a Monday and I have breakfast for the rest of the week. This method is unparalleled and you should try it.

Help! You said I could flavor my oats by adding whatever I want, but what does that mean???

Sugar, syrup, herbs, spices, nuts, fruit, seeds… You can just go on youtube and type “oatmeal recipe” or “overnight oats recipe” and you will find hundreds of videos for inspiration. Here are two simple things I like to do:

Boiled Oats

  • Once they’re done cooking, I like to add brown sugar, a bit of cinnamon, and maple syrup. Just add as much as you like until it tastes good to you!

Overnight Oats

  • I like to make chocolate oats by adding:
  • cocoa powder
  • sugar
  • maple syrup
  • Chocolate chips (optional)
  • Be sure to mix it all together, do a quick taste test and adjust as you like. Keep in mind it will have a mushier texture in the morning.

Bonus Tips!!!!

  • Add chia seeds for a bit of extra nutrition and fiber! Very small and flavorless, you won’t even notice they’re there! I like to do 1 tablespoon of chia seeds per 1 cup of oats
  • Overnight oats are very mushy, so I like to add crunchy elements to help break up the monotony. Nuts and chocolate chips are good because they don’t soak up much liquid and thus don’t become mushy like everything else.
